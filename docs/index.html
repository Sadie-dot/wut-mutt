Wut Mutt

The Breeds of Our Lives.

Wut Mutt guesses the breed mix of a dog from a photo, and stages the answer as a 1980s TV soap opera: a dramatic entrance, a cliffhanger analysis, a DNA-test reveal, and a gossip card you can share. Photograph a dog that isn't a dog and you get your own shocking-twist episode.

Support

Questions, bug reports, or breed disputes — email sadief@gmail.com and a human will read it.

Common questions

  • Is the breed guess accurate? It's an educated guess for entertainment, generated by Claude from your photo — not veterinary, genetic, or dramatic advice. For real answers, ask a vet or a DNA test.
  • Why does REVEAL stay dim? The app waits until it can see a dog in the frame. Back up so the whole animal is visible — a head filling the frame is harder to spot than a whole dog.
  • "The studio goes dark until tomorrow." That's the daily reveal limit. It resets the next day.
  • Can I use my own Anthropic API key? Yes — the app accepts one, and it's stored only in your device's Keychain.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. The short version: no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and the only thing that leaves your device is a photo you choose to have identified.
